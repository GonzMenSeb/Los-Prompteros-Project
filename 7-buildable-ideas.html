The seven

Grounding is shown at two depths — the short version by default, the full stack behind “See implementation details.” The verification protocol and the tool checklist are the parts that matter for the rubric's rigor.

What "verified by me" means & doesn't. A ✓ means I sent a real request from my environment and got real data back at test time — it is not a guarantee of uptime, rate limits, or terms-of-use for a hackathon. A ✗ is usually an environment limit (e.g. a domain my sandbox couldn't resolve), not proof the tool is dead — re-check those from your own network. Endpoints, especially undocumented storefront ones, can change without notice; confirm the ones you depend on the morning of.
